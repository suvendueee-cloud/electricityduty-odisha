1. Monthly Report — adding each month's dues

One month at a time: pick the month in the Month box, type the Current month dues, then click Add month. Do this once for every month you have — the report recalculates automatically each time.

Many months at once (bulk upload): first pick the earliest month in the Month box — this tells the ledger which month the first figure belongs to. Then click Upload Excel (Col A = Current Month ED) and choose a spreadsheet with the dues listed one below another down column A, starting at cell A1. A1 becomes the month you picked, A2 the next month, A3 the one after, and so on — no header row, no dates in the file, just the numbers in order.

2. Payment Report — recording what's been paid

One payment at a time: pick the Date paid, type the ED paid amount, then click Add payment.

Many payments at once (bulk upload): click Upload Excel (Col A = Date, Col B = Payment) and choose a spreadsheet where column A holds the payment date and column B holds the matching amount, row by row — A1 with B1, A2 with B2, and so on. Each payment is added with its own exact date from the file, so there's nothing to correct afterward. This one doesn't need a month picked first — the dates come from the file itself.

3. Ledger Settings — opening amounts and interest rate

Open Ledger settings (the gold bar near the top, or the "edit ledger settings" link under the title) to set:

  • Consumer name — shown throughout the ledger and in the PDF.
  • Yearly interest rate (%) — the simple-interest rate applied daily for the whole ledger.
  • Opening carry-forward interest, opening arrear ED, opening previous-month due — the three starting balances that apply on the 1st day of your earliest month. Everything after that is carried forward automatically.

Changing any of these recalculates the entire ledger immediately.

4. Save Ledger and Import Saved Ledger

This page keeps your data only in the browser tab you're using — nothing is saved automatically to a server. To keep your work:

  • Save Ledger downloads everything — settings, months, and payments — as a single .json file.
  • Import Saved Ledger loads a .json file you saved earlier, restoring the ledger exactly as it was.

Both buttons are in the Ledger settings drawer. Save regularly, especially before closing the tab.

5. What the Complete Report shows

The Complete Report brings the Monthly Report and Payment Report together into one month-by-month view: each row shows that month's Current Month dues, Prev Month, Arrear, Interest figures, every individual payment received that month (with how it was split between Interest, Arrear, Prev Month, Current, and Extra), and the resulting Balance. Below the table, the Outstanding Summary shows the total still owed as of the latest month, split into Interest and Arrear/Principal, plus how much of all payments made went toward interest. This is also the only tab that can be turned into a PDF, using its Save as PDF button.
